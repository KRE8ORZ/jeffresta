Ballet Photography

Jeff Resta specializes in ballet photography, dance photography and performing arts photography. Based in Dallas, Texas, his work has documented ballet companies, dance productions and artistic performances throughout the region.

Jeff served as House Photographer for Texas Ballet Theater, creating photography that captures the artistry, athleticism and visual storytelling of live ballet performance.

Texas Ballet Theater

A significant portion of Jeff's performing arts portfolio includes photography created in association with Texas Ballet Theater. His work documents principal dancers, company performances, special productions and the visual artistry that defines professional ballet.

Dance and Performing Arts Photography

Ballet photography presents unique challenges involving motion, stage lighting, timing and composition. Jeff's approach focuses on preserving the movement, emotion and technical precision of dance while maintaining the visual atmosphere of live performance.

His photography seeks to celebrate the artistry of dancers, choreographers and performing arts organizations while creating lasting visual records of important performances and productions.
