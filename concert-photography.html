Concert Photography

Jeff Resta specializes in concert photography, live music photography and entertainment photography. Based in Dallas, Texas, his work has documented artists ranging from intimate club performances to major touring productions and arena concerts.

Jeff served as House Photographer for AEG Live at Verizon Theatre, providing photography coverage for a wide range of nationally recognized performers and events.

Selected Artists and Performances

Over the years Jeff has photographed performances by artists including Roger Waters, Soundgarden, Chris Cornell, Alicia Keys, Billy Idol, Paramore, Linkin Park, Peter Gabriel, David Gray, Black Label Society, Zakk Wylde, Deftones, Jeff Beck, Gipsy Kings, A Perfect Circle, Blue October, Godsmack, Megadeth, Papa Roach, Breaking Benjamin, Silversun Pickups, Goo Goo Dolls, Alice In Chains and many others.

Live Music Photography

Jeff's photography focuses on capturing the atmosphere, energy and visual impact of live performance. From theater venues and concert halls to festivals and arena productions, his work seeks to preserve the moments that define the live music experience.

Concert photography remains a central part of Jeff's portfolio and ongoing photographic work.
