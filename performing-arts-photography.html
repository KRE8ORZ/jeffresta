Performing Arts Photography

Jeff Resta specializes in performing arts photography, documenting ballet, theater, dance and live artistic performance. Based in Dallas, Texas, his work focuses on preserving the visual impact and atmosphere of live productions.

Through years of work with performing arts organizations and cultural institutions, Jeff has photographed a wide variety of productions ranging from classical ballet and contemporary dance to theatrical performances and large-scale entertainment productions.

Documenting Live Performance

Performing arts photography requires an understanding of movement, timing, stage lighting and composition. Jeff's work seeks to capture the moments that define each production while preserving the artistry and creativity of the performers and organizations involved.

From ballet and dance to theatrical productions and cultural events, his photography is dedicated to celebrating the visual power of live performance.
